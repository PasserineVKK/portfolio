**May 2026 · Personal case study adapted from a Coursera Specialization scenario**

I evaluated an English language center's attendance workflow, where formal LMS assumptions did not match the communication practices used day to day.

**My work**

- Tested initial assumptions through contextual observation at an English center.
- Identified the gap created by absences reported outside the LMS.
- Compared LMS-only and teacher-controlled correction approaches, their feasibility and trade-offs; defined three key business rules.
- Produced as-is/to-be UML activity diagrams, annotated wireframes and documented limitations.
- Designed a workflow that reduces incorrect unexcused absences while retaining compliance tracking and an audit trail.

**Skills and tools:** Contextual observation, informal elicitation, requirements validation, gap analysis, process modeling, wireframing, Draw.io, Mermaid, Google Stitch and Google Workspace.

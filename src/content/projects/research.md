**FISAT 2026 · Sep 2026 · Full paper · Systematic literature review**

The review examines elicitation, analysis, specification, validation and management. It asks whether an end-to-end AI-assisted process is feasible and how AI-generated requirements should be evaluated.

**First-author contribution:** I proposed the topic and research questions, structured the paper, wrote the synthesis method and conclusions, and led evidence extraction from secondary studies.

**What I learned:** Correctness, consistency and traceability are central requirement quality criteria throughout the Requirements Engineering lifecycle.

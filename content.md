### EduConnect LMS: Attendance & Leave Management Feature

- **Time:** May 2026 | **Type:** Personal project / case study report (scenario adapted from Coursera's Specialization)
- **Role:** Business Analyst
- **Link:** [Case Study Report](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SwCPBVuL7uyQyvmsr8EktXWD9mlgXeIe/view?usp=sharing)
- **Description:** A case study evaluating and refining the LMS attendance and leave management workflow for an English language center, addressing the gap between formal system assumptions and actual communication practices (Zalo, verbal).
- **Main tasks:**
  - Tested initial LMS assumptions through contextual observation at an English center
  - Performed gap analysis to define the core problem of uncaptured external absences

  - Designed a workflow capturing external absences while preserving an audit trail
- **Skills:** Contextual observation, informal elicitation, requirements validation, gap analysis, process modeling (UML activity diagram), wireframing & prototyping
- **Tools:** Draw.io, Mermaid, Google Stitch, Google Workspace
- **Result:** A validated hybrid attendance workflow that minimizes incorrectly recorded unexcused absences and maintains compliance tracking while accommodating external communication channels
- **Learning:** Using comparative analysis to evaluate trade-offs, and extracting business requirements through contextual observation and informal communication
